South Park’s “Post Covid” season inspired me to create an interface for the app of the personal assistant of the future.

Imagine we are in 2040 and everyone has their own personal assistant, that not necessarily is humanified as in the South Park series, but it is completely normal to have one in your pocket and to use it instead of paying people to do stuff for you or instead of you. Imagine your virtual assistant is not just about timers, music, and Amazon purchases. It is also your therapist, best friend, secretary, or partner as in the movie “Her”.

Imagine AI gathers all the information not just for giving you shit in advertisements to buy, but for more useful purposes. Useful for you. For the futuristic app project provided by my “Muse Case Labs” boot camp, I decided to combine the virtual assistant and emotional intelligence in one app.
